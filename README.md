# Bunkit

Track lecture attendance and see how many lectures you can safely bunk. Works offline, data stays on your device unless you choose to sign in and sync.

Created by Kunsh S Agarwal - [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/kunshsumitagarwal) - [Instagram](https://instagram.com/kunshsumitagarwal)

## Privacy

- Your attendance, subjects and name are saved on your own device.
- If you sign in with Google, your data is also saved to your private cloud copy so it syncs across your devices. Only you can read it.
- The app collects anonymous usage statistics (for example, which tabs are used). Your name, subjects, email and attendance are never included. You can turn this off in the Account tab, and browsers set to Do Not Track are skipped.
